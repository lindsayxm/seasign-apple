# v5 — HUD

Today as a futuristic head-up display, after `design-ideas/ref-img/v5-hud/night.png`. Look only; no app code.

- One dial that stays where it is. Its colour and brightness carry the time: reds, oranges and yellows on black by day, deep aqua to white on black at night.
- A marker on the rim shows how far the sun or moon is through its day (left, over the top, to the right). The moon's phase is the lit part of the disc. Weather is a sign and a word, and marks inside the dial.
- `index.html` is the animated mockup. Open it in a browser (`open design-ideas/v5-hud/index.html`) and pick a state, or step through with the arrow keys.
- `01-sunrise.png` … `12-moon-set.png` are stills of the same twelve states as `design-ideas/v1-original/`, at iPhone size.
- `fonts/` holds a copy of the app's Jost font so the page works offline.
