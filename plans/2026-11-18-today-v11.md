Date: 2026-11-18. Owner: lead session. Tasks: #145 (scene), #146 (overlay).

## Scope

- The Today screen only. Settings, Do/Get, Saved, History and Reading keep their present look. Do not change `Palette` values or type styles those screens use; add new ones.
- No API change. `/v1/sky` already gives everything (`windSpeedMph`, cloud cover, weather code, rise and set instants, moon phase).
- The mockup's clock and home bar are the phone's own; do not draw them.
- The old design's wind marks (dashes on the water, streaked reflection) go; wind is now a number.
- Kept behaviour: the zip prompt, the no-sites prompt, the AI mark on AI-found items, error and status lines, fewer than three events, the phone's 12- or 24-hour setting, 44-point touch targets, accessibility labels, Reduce Motion (one still frame), the DEBUG scene override, cache-first loading.

## Geometry (mockup points; multiply by `SceneLayout.scale` = width / 393)

- Horizon `HZ` at y 386.
- Path of the sun and moon: the top segment of a circle whose centre is under the horizon. Centre x = width / 2, half-width 134.5, top 88 above the horizon. `r = (half² + top²) / (2·top)`, `a0 = asin((r − top) / r)`, and at progress `u` (0 rising, 1 setting): `q = π − a0 − u(π − 2·a0)`, `x = cx + r·cos q`, `y = HZ + r − top − r·sin q`. `u` is the existing rise-to-set fraction.
- `lowness = max(0, 1 − (HZ − y) / top)²`. Body radius `R = 12 · (1 + 3.2 · lowness)`: 12 high in the sky, about 50 on the horizon. The horizon hides the part of the body below it.
- One body at a time, as now: the sun while it is up, otherwise the moon while it is up, otherwise none.

## Looks on real data

The mockup has discrete looks; the app picks by these rules (cross-fade where cheap, otherwise switch):

| look | when |
|---|---|
| sunrise | sun up and `u < 0.12` |
| sunset | sun up and `u > 0.88` |
| day | sun up otherwise; `cloudy`, `rain`, `snow` variants by the existing `CloudLevel` and `Precipitation` |
| dusk | sun down and within the existing twilight (40 minutes of a sunrise or sunset), or the moon within `u < 0.12` / `u > 0.88` |
| night | sun down otherwise |
| full | night with the moon more than 97% lit |

- **Ink colour** (one per screen; everything highlighted uses it): sunrise `255,244,190`; sunset `255,205,186`; day and its weather variants `244,216,104`; all night looks `150,232,240`.
- **Stars**: at full strength whenever the sun is down. With the sun up they show only in the sunrise and sunset looks and in cloud, rain or snow; a clear day sky away from sunrise and sunset has none. (This mirrors the mockup, where only the clear mid-day screen lost its stars at the user's request. It is flagged to the user as a question.)
- **Day sky**, sunrise and sunset bands, sea tint, veils, grain, bloom: as `draw(t)`.
- **Weather** is gathered round the body (within about 96 points), clipped above the horizon: clouds as flat wide soft bands, rain as fine slanting lines, snow as small dots. With no body up, centre it on the middle of the path.
- **Neutral scene** (no zip, or a stale sky): as now, a clear day on the clock (06–18) with no temperature; draw the sun on the path from that clock, and no ticks, times, tag or wind.

## Marks tied to the sun, moon and horizon

Drawn after the bloom so they stay crisp, before the grain.

- **Hourly ticks** hang under the horizon between the body's rise point and set point (x 62 and 331): one per hour of the real span between this pass's rise and set instants (`round(hours)` intervals), 3 points long, 7 at the two ends. White 42%, ends 70%.
- **Rise and set times** in small mono type (8.5 pt, white 95%, dark shadow) centred under the two end ticks, 13 points below the horizon, in the phone's clock style (`6:41 AM`, or `06:41`). If a rise or set instant is unknown, leave out that time and its end tick.
- **Square**: 4 × 4, ink colour, on the horizon under the body, when `0.05 < u < 0.95`.
- **Drop line**: dotted, white 40%, from 11 points under the body to the horizon, when the body's lower edge is more than 14 points above the horizon.
- **Tag**: only when `u < 0.05` or `u > 0.95`. Two lines of mono 8.5 pt. First line in the ink colour: `SUNRISE`, `SUNSET`, `MOONRISE` or `MOONSET` and that time. Second line white: `WIND n MPH` (rounded; left out when there is no fresh current weather). It sits above the body on a short bent leader, to the right of a rising body and to the left of a setting one; see `show()` and the leader in `draw(t)` for the offsets. The time mark under that end is hidden while the tag shows.
- No brackets round the body. No tag or leader away from the horizon.

## Fixed overlay (task #146)

All x from the left edge, y from the top of the screen, in mockup points.

- **Left column, all white `238,240,240`**:
  - date `NOV 16`: Jost 9 pt, tracking .26 em, at x 34;
  - day name `MON`: Avenir Next Condensed Regular (ships with iOS), sized so its width equals the date's width (about 28 pt), with a soft white glow;
  - temperature `55°F`: Jost 10 pt, tracking .2 em. Left out when unknown.
  - The top of the date's letters is level with the top of the first event's text; the bottom of the temperature is level with the bottom of the last event's text; the day name sits midway.
- **Events box**: x 96 to 377, top 64, black 55%, padding 8. Up to three events, 10 points apart. Each has a 1-point rule in the ink colour (60%) at its left, then the time in mono 10 pt in the ink colour as two fixed columns (hour, then `AM` / `PM`; an all-day event shows the day and `ALL`), then the title in Jost 11 pt capitals, tracking .13 em, line height 16, wrapping as now. 24-hour phones show one column.
- **Hairline**: white 34%, half a point, x 34 to 377, along the bottom edge of the events box (y 168 with the sample text), and never less than 16 points under the temperature. Everything below hangs from it:
  - **wind line** `WIND 8 MPH`: mono 8.5 pt, white 92%, at x 34, the top of its letters 18 points under the hairline (level with the top of the settings button). Shown only when the tag is not shown and fresh current weather exists.
  - **three buttons** (settings, list, saved): 19-point line icons at 38% of the ink colour, centred on x 331, tops 18, 63 and 108 points under the hairline. 44-point targets, present labels.
- **Article titles**: from x 34, first row at y 486 (100 under the horizon), rows 57 points apart, at most five and only as many as fit above `∀` (`HeadlineFit`). Each: a number `01`… in mono 8 pt, ink colour 85%, in a 23-point column; the title in Iowan Old Style (ships with iOS) 13.5 pt, line height 17.5, colour `244,242,236`, sentence case as published, up to two lines. The AI mark and prompts keep their meaning.
- **`∀`** (all publications; replaces `»`): the path `M.08 .04 L.5 .96 L.92 .04 M.226 .36 H.774` in a 10-point box, line width 13% of the box, ink colour at full strength, centred on the screen's width, its top 58 points above the bottom of the screen (clear of the home bar). 44-point target; opens the 7-day history as now.

## Technical notes

- The mockup uses additive blending, a whole-frame blur for the soft light, a grain tile and 2,600 stars per frame. Look up `GraphicsContext` (blend modes, `addFilter`, `drawLayer`, resolved images) in the `sosumi` docs first. Pre-render what does not change every frame (star field, grain tile, static gradients). Judge by side-by-side likeness to the stills, not pixel match, and keep the frame rate smooth on both runtimes.
- Guard anything newer than iOS 18 with `if #available`.
- Keep pure rules (path, radius, looks, ink, star strength, tick count, tag and wind-line conditions, labels) in `SkyMath` / `SceneState` with tests; keep the renderer free of decisions.
- The DEBUG override already has `sunrise`, `midday`, `sunset`, `night`, `moonrise`, `moonset`, a moon phase and `clear|cloudy|windy|rain|snow`; extend it only as needed to reach the twelve states.
