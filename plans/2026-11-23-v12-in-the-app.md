## Defaults the lead chose where the mockup is silent (told to the user; theirs to change)

- **Events box:** four rows (Today showed three). Each row is as many characters as fit the box at the mono size: time right-aligned in five, `A` or `P`, dashes, the title, a slash; a title too long is cut and ends in the slash. An all-day event today shows `ALL` in the time column and no letter. An event on another day shows the weekday (`TUE`) in the time column and no letter. This is a pure, tested formatter. The spoken label is the plain event (the existing `EventLabel` wording), never the dashes.
- **States the mockup does not draw:** no events, the zip prompt, a failed calendar, no place name, fewer than five titles. Behaviour stays as it is now, drawn in the new style (accent mono text in the same places). With no place name the top right shows only settings.
- **Other screen sizes:** side margins stay 34 points and the tiles stretch. The scene keeps the proportions `TodayOverlayLayout` already uses. On a short screen (iPhone SE) the titles give way first: only whole two-line rows that fit above the tiles are shown. On a tall screen the spare height goes to the scene.
- **Rim:** follows the device's corner radius where the system reports one; the mockup's 39 points is the fallback.
- **Lifecycle:** when another tab is showing, Today's scene animation and its refresh loops pause exactly as they do now under a cover, and resume on return.
- **SAVE tile:** a tap switches to Saved; a drag of its star onto a title saves that title, as now.
- **Fonts that fall out of use** (Jost, PT Sans Narrow) stay in the bundle until the user says to remove them.

## Colour rule

The stored value is one hue (degrees), optional; nil means the default orange-red `rgb(235, 78, 39)`. A pure function turns a hue into the accent colour by holding relative luminance inside a band:

- floor: WCAG contrast of at least 4.5:1 for small accent text on the dark plate (`rgb(8, 9, 12)`), and at least 4.5:1 for the dark text on a filled tile (both need the accent bright enough);
- ceiling: at least 3:1 against the white date tile, so a filled tile never reads as the date tile.

As built in task 192: every hue is held at the default orange-red's own luminance (0.2326), inside the band 0.1898 to 0.2630. Over the sky the accent is not covered by this rule (the glow round the sun or moon is about as bright as the accent); there the labels keep the mockup's dark text shadow.

Tested for every whole degree. The default orange-red must pass its own rule; if it does not, the band is wrong, not the default.
