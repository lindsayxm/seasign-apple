# Decisions of 2026-11-23: v12-imago replaces v11 in the app

Made by the user in the lead session. The plan is `plans/2026-11-23-v12-in-the-app.md`.

## v12 is the app's design

- **Decision:** the Today screen is rebuilt after `design-ideas/v12-imago/` ("It should look just as the mockup looks"). The scene (sky, sea, sun, moon, weather) stays v11's. The other screens are restyled lightly to match, layouts kept.
- **Replaces:** `plans/2026-11-18-today-v11.md` as the reference for Today's buttons and words. `ref-img/` no longer governs the other screens' look where it conflicts with v12.

## Navigation: a four-tile tab bar

- **Decision:** DAY (Today), LIST (Do / Get), SAVE (Saved) and LISTEN are tabs; the four tiles stay at the bottom of those four pages and the filled one is the page showing. Settings (top right of Today), the full-day calendar, the read view and History open over them with a close mark.
- **Rejected:** tiles on Today only, with the pages as full-screen covers as before (the lead's recommendation); tiles under every screen.
- History (`∀`, the last 7 days) is opened from Saved, not from Today.
- LISTEN is a placeholder page that says "Coming later." What it will be is not decided.

## HUD colour is the user's choice, within limits

- **Decision:** Settings has one hue slider. The app sets brightness and saturation for each hue so the accent always passes a contrast check against the dark panels, the sky and the filled tile's dark text. Default: orange-red `rgb(235, 78, 39)`.
- The accent changes on every screen. The white parts (mark, date tile, temperature and wind, corner brackets, rise and set times) stay white.
- **Rejected:** preset swatches; Apple's full colour picker with correction; a colour for Today only.

## Type

- **Decision:** the system mono face for the HUD on every screen; article text in the read view keeps a reading face. No font is bundled for v12.
- **Rejected:** Nulshock (the folder `fonts/` at the repository root is the user's, untracked, and must not be committed: its licence is a desktop licence).

## Today's content

- The weather word and the moon's phase name are no longer shown on Today; the scene carries them.
- Four events are shown (was three).
- The day calendar's mic and + stay in the header row with the close mark.
