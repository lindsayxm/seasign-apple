# 2026-11-24: the user's four open choices from the v12 build

Asked at the start of the session of 2026-11-24 (later); each answer was the lead's recommended option.

- **The iPhone SE shows two feed titles.** A screen that fits only one title row at the 54-point pitch gets a tighter pitch so a second fits (50 on the SE, never under 46). Other phones do not change. Task 207.
- **The color block in Settings needs a short press.** A swipe that starts on the block scrolls the page; the block picks a color after a short press (about 0.15 s), with or without a drag, or on a tap. Why: a scroll that started on the block changed the HUD color by accident. Task 209.
- **Dark yellows and greens along the block's bottom edge stay as they are.** Every hue on the bottom edge is held at the default orange-red's brightness, and bright yellows and greens are reached by moving up the block. No task.
- **The now line shows across the filled event on the day screen.** The piece of the line inside the filled box is drawn on the fill, under the text, in the color of the box's text (white would be lost on a light accent). Outlined boxes still hide the line. Task 208.
